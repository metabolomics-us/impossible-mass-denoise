How it works

  1. Read. Your file is read in small pieces and cut into whole spectra, so even a 1 GB file is never held in memory all at once.
  2. Check. Each ion is tested against every formula built from carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, oxygen, phosphorus and sulfur (optionally halogens) at its mass.
  3. Clean. Ions that no such formula explains are removed. Everything else in the record is kept exactly as it was, in the same format.
What does “impossible” mean?

Impossible means not explained by CHNOPS, singly charged, monoisotopic. Works for Orbitrap data; off by default for TTOF; not for QTOF. Ions above 1700 m/z are always reported possible.
